What is
Self-Love?

Self-love is the act of cultivating practices that help us stay connected to our heart. From this space we live with more compassion, trust, and devotion to ourselves.

Practising self-love is an empowering act of heart connection. It involves treating yourself with kindness, respect, and compassion while accepting all your flaws and imperfections.

By prioritising your own well-being and recognising your own worth, you can offer yourself the same care and understanding that you would provide to a dear friend in need. Self-love is not about being selfish or arrogant — it is about loving and valuing yourself just as you are.

The Heart
Chakra

Anahata — translates to "unstruck" or "unhurt". Located in the middle of the chest, just above the physical heart.

The heart chakra is responsible for the distribution of Prana (life force energy) throughout the body. It expands all around the body creating an electric field that radiates around 6 feet from the body. It is responsible for emotions related to love, compassion, forgiveness, empathy, and acceptance — associated with the air element, signifying openness, freedom, and expansion.

When balanced

Feelings of love, compassion, empathy, and connection with oneself and others. A sense of inner peace, harmony, and acceptance. Relationships characterised by mutual respect, understanding, and support.

When out of balance

Feelings of loneliness, bitterness, jealousy, or an inability to forgive. Physical symptoms such as heart palpitations, respiratory issues, or circulation problems may also indicate imbalance.

Balancing practices: Gratitude practices, heart opening yoga, heart opening meditations, breathwork, spending time in nature, affirmations, having boundaries, listening to your intuition, connecting to God/source/the universe, intentional movement, and focusing on presence in daily life.

How to Cultivate
Self-Love

Learning to cultivate self-love can be a long journey, but it is an important one for our mental and emotional well-being. Be kind and compassionate towards yourself, especially during challenging times.

Become aware of your inner dialogue

An important step is becoming aware of your inner dialogue and challenging negative self-talk. Replace self-criticism with self-affirmations that reinforce your worth and capabilities.

Boundary setting

Setting boundaries is a crucial aspect of cultivating self-love. Learn to say no to commitments, requests, and relationships that drain you or compromise your well-being. It is okay to prioritise yourself.

Prioritise self-care

Prioritise self-care as a non-negotiable aspect of your daily routine. Dedicate time to activities that nourish your body, mind, and soul — exercise, meditation, hobbies, quality time with loved ones.

Seek external support

Surround yourself with supportive people who uplift and encourage you. Seek out communities of like-minded people who understand and value the importance of self-love. Professional support is a form of self-love too.

How Self-Love
Impacts Your Life

Self-love can have a radical impact across all aspects of our lives.

Emotional wellbeing

Promotes mental resilience and emotional well-being, reducing stress and equipping you with inner strength and confidence to navigate challenges with grace.

Self-compassion

When you have a deep sense of self-love, you become better equipped to navigate challenges, setbacks, and failures with compassion rather than self-criticism.

Self-empowerment

Self-love empowers you to assert yourself and pursue your goals and dreams, leading to greater success and fulfilment in your personal and professional life.

Improved relationships

Self-love improves your relationships by helping you set healthy boundaries, communicate assertively, and cultivate fulfilling relationships based on mutual respect.

Diaphragmatic Breath Practice

01Inhale slowly (ideally through the nose) and visualise the air travelling all the way down toward your pelvis, filling up your belly, then your chest, until you cannot take any more air into your lungs.
02Exhale by simply relaxing your ribcage and diaphragm, allowing the air to slowly fall out of the chest without any force.
03Repeat this breathing style for several rounds and notice how your internal state shifts.
After the breath practice — what do you notice?

Eye-Gazing Meditation

  1. Set your space with loving intentions. Light a candle or your favourite incense.
  2. Set yourself up in front of a mirror.
  3. Simply begin to look at yourself in the mirror, eventually meeting your own gaze.
  4. Allow yourself to look into the depths of your soul through your own eyes.
  5. Stay as long as feels right. Be patient. Welcome in all emotions.

This practice can bring a lot of emotions to the surface. Be sure to journal about your experience afterwards.
