The problem

You are posting. You are showing up. People aren't booking.

The instinct is to post more, try different platforms, or change your content style. Usually that is not the problem. The problem is that your offer, your message, or your sign-up pathway is not clear enough yet.

Genuine Reach works through all three — with practical frameworks you can apply to your retreat, workshop, or practice the same day. No algorithm hacks. No performing. Just your work, communicated clearly.

This guide is for you if
  • You are posting content that gets engagement but no sign-ups — and you want to understand the gap
  • You are a retreat host who knows the experience is transformational but struggle to communicate that in a way that converts
  • You have an offering but feel like your message isn't reaching the right people
  • You want to grow your audience without losing your voice or your values
  • You are tired of marketing advice that was designed for product businesses, not healing work
The approach

Positioning first. Marketing second.

No amount of content strategy fixes unclear positioning. This guide starts with your offer and your message — and once that is clear, builds simple marketing workflows designed specifically for retreats and events, not generic product businesses.

Your offering — clarity first

What are you actually offering and what transformation does it create? Before marketing anything, we need clarity on the work itself.

Who it's for

Clarity comes from specificity. Who benefits most from this work, and what are they currently struggling with?

Simplifying your message

What is it? Who is it for? Why does it matter? Often less is more effective. This section helps you distil your message to its most powerful form.

Your sign-up pathway

Most practitioners don't have a clear pathway from interest to booking. Map your client journey and remove the friction that is costing you bookings.

Marketing workflows for retreats

Simple systems you can set up in an afternoon — email sequences, social strategy, launch timelines. Designed for events and retreats, not generic businesses.

One focus, next steps

Clarity without action changes nothing. This section helps you identify the one thing to focus on first — and actually do it.
