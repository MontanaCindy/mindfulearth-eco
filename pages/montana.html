The person behind the practice

Not a service menu.
A human being.

I am Canadian-born, currently living in London, and I have been in this work since 2019 — when Mindful Earth started as a bracelet business with a simple belief that caring for people and caring for the planet are the same work.

I have a BSc in life sciences, 200 hours of yoga teacher training, 300 hours of somatic bodywork certification, and years of retreat hosting internationally — in Nicaragua, in Canada, and increasingly in Europe. I say all of that not to impress, but because I know practitioners and retreat hosts want to know who they are working with.

What matters more to me is this: I have been the person who needed healing and could not afford it. And I have been the practitioner trying to build something sustainable while pouring everything into others. Both of those experiences live in how I work.

I am in my own Saturn Return. I have relocated twice in two years. I know what it is to lose your sense of who you are and have to find your way back to yourself in a new city, with a new version of your life. I am not someone who has it all figured out. I am someone who has learned to keep returning — to the body, to the earth, to the people who matter.

That is what I bring into the room.

What to Expect

Not a session.
A shift.

Every person who works with me is different — their path, their pace, their readiness. I work with a small number of people at a time by design. This work deserves presence, not a production line.

01

To feel genuinely seen — not as a client, but as a whole person with a specific story and a specific path forward.

02

To be challenged — gently, honestly, and only ever in the direction of your own growth.

03

To leave more connected to yourself — your body, your purpose, your community, your place in this world.

04

To carry something that stays — a shift in how you move through life, not just how you feel for a day.
