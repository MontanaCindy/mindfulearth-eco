A bit about me

I built this from the
inside out.

BSc Life Sciences 200hr YTT 300hr Somatic Certification 6 Years International Retreats

I am Canadian — born and raised — and moved to London in 2025 not entirely knowing why. Only that something needed to shift, and sometimes you have to change the backdrop before you can change anything else. I brought my dog. I am still figuring the rest out.

I have lived with anxiety since I was six years old. It has shaped everything — the way I move through a room, the work I chose, the practitioner I became. A lot of what I do now is rooted in what I had to learn the hard way: how to regulate a nervous system that wanted to keep me small, how to work with fear rather than around it, how to show up anyway. Yoga found me at eighteen and hasn't left.

The past few years have held more grief than I knew how to carry. Loss has a way of doing that — making you a stranger to yourself. I am only just now finding my way back. I share this not for sympathy, but because I think it matters for you to know that the person you are working with has been in the dark too. Has sat with the kind of pain that doesn't go away, only changes shape. I am learning to live alongside mine, and I want to help you do the same.

I built Mindful Earth from all of that. From the belief that people should be able to do the things they dream of — host the retreat, build the brand, step into the role — and that there should be real, honest support available when they are ready to try. I love the inner work of it. The fear that comes with putting something into the world. The identity shifts that come with stepping into leadership. I have been through all of it. That is what I bring to this.

I believe in something larger than myself. I pray, not in a religious sense, but in the way of someone who is still on the journey — still figuring out what it means to trust, to surrender, to be held by something you cannot quite name. That spirituality is woven into everything I do, quietly.

One of my biggest dreams is to have retreat venues in different countries. Places I have visited enough times to love, and love enough to want to share. I am working toward that, one event at a time.

Work With Montana

Where do you want
to begin?

Working with me is for you if you are somewhere in the middle of building something — a retreat, a brand, a practice — and it feels bigger than you expected. If you are scared of getting it wrong. If you have the vision but not the structure. If you are doing the inner work and the outer work at the same time and it is a lot.

You have come to the right place. I know how it feels to stand at the beginning of something and not know where to start. I know the particular overwhelm of trying to plan an event, market it honestly, and still show up as a whole person. I have been there — many times. I am still there, in different ways.

I work with the nervous system. I work with fear and anxiety. I work with the stories that keep people from stepping into what they are here to do. And I work with the practical stuff too — the logistics, the marketing, the brand. All of it, together, because that is the only way it actually works.

No pressure. No wrong entry point. Everything starts with a free thirty-minute call.

What to Expect

Not a session.
A shift.

Every person who works with me is different — their path, their pace, their readiness. I work with a small number of people at a time by design. This work deserves presence, not a production line.

01

To feel genuinely seen — not as a client, but as a whole person with a specific story and a specific path forward.

02

To be challenged — gently, honestly, and only ever in the direction of your own growth.

03

To leave more connected to yourself — your body, your purpose, your community, your place in this world.

04

To carry something that stays — a shift in how you move through life, not just how you feel for a day.
