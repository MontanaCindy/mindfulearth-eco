What this is really about

Self-love is not a destination. It's a practice you return to.

Most self-love content asks you to feel something you don't, or perform a version of yourself you haven't found yet. This workbook does not do that. It meets you where you are — in the grief, the exhaustion, the self-doubt, the confusion — and offers a way back to yourself that is honest rather than performative.

This is the companion for the seasons when showing up for yourself feels hard. It is not about fixing yourself. It is about remembering you are worth returning to.

This guide is for you if
  • You are in a season of loss, transition, or rediscovery and feel a bit lost in it
  • You are harder on yourself than you would ever be on someone you love
  • You want a reflective practice but journaling prompts that say "what are you grateful for?" feel hollow
  • You are doing the work — therapy, yoga, somatic sessions — and want something to work with between sessions
  • You want to come home to yourself, and you are tired of being told it should be easy
What's inside

50+ pages of honest, warm, non-linear practice.

Journaling prompts, reflection practices, seasonal check-ins, and somatic awareness exercises. Written with care, without judgment, and with the understanding that returning to yourself happens slowly — and in spirals, not straight lines.

Where you are right now

An honest starting point. Not where you should be — where you actually are. Meeting yourself there without the need to immediately change it.

The inner critic

Understanding where your self-criticism comes from — and beginning to relate to it differently. Not silencing it. Getting curious about it.

Practices for coming home

Somatic awareness exercises, journaling rituals, and daily micro-practices for returning to yourself — especially on the days when you feel furthest away.

Seasonal check-ins

This is not a workbook you do once. Seasonal prompts invite you back as you change — because who you are returning to is always slightly different.

Grief and loss

A section for the harder seasons — when self-love feels impossible because you are in the middle of something genuinely hard. Permission to be there. Tools for being in it.

Coming back to your body

Gentle somatic practices for reconnecting with your physical self — especially useful for anyone who has spent years living above the neck.
