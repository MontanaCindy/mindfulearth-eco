The work

Art as medicine.
Ceremony as home.

Tammy Roggie is an abstract artist, medicine woman, and ceremony holder based in Ontario, Canada. Her practice lives at the intersection of the visual and the ancestral — where a painting is not decoration but invocation, and where ceremony creates the kind of container that changes people in ways they often cannot articulate until weeks later.

Her abstract work carries an energetic signature — made to be lived with, returned to, felt. Each piece is an encounter. Her ceremony work is grounded in ancestral practice and shaped entirely around what is needed in the room — she does not bring a script, she brings presence.

Tammy has collaborated with retreat hosts and wellness practitioners to bring ceremony and visual art into multi-day experiences — adding a depth and dimension that participants consistently describe as the part of the retreat that stayed with them longest.

Based in Ontario, Canada. Available online and in person for ceremony, workshops, and retreats. Willing to travel for the right collaboration.

Abstract Painting

Original works and prints that carry an energetic signature — made to be lived with, not just looked at. Available for purchase and commission.

Ceremony & Ritual

Bespoke ceremony for individuals, groups, and retreats. Rooted in ancestral practice and shaped around what is needed in the room.

Retreat Collaboration

Available to collaborate with retreat hosts — bringing ceremony and art practice into multi-day wellness experiences. In person and online.
