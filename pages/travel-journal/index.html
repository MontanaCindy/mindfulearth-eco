Montana Cindy

A travel journal.

Places, retreats, moments. An honest record of where the work has taken me and what I have found along the way.

"One of my biggest dreams is to have retreat venues in different countries. Places I have been to enough times to know — and love enough to want to share. This journal is the beginning of that map."

— Montana Cindy
Nicaragua · February 2025

San Juan del Sur. The kind of place that gets into you. We arrived as strangers and left as something more — the particular closeness that only forms when people are willing to be real with each other for a week straight. There was surf and yoga and late dinners and something that felt like ceremony. I have been hosting retreats for years and this one reminded me exactly why I started.

Cedar Mindful Retreat · 7 days · Sold out
Canmore, Alberta · Canada

Home, in a different sense than London. The Rockies are where I learned to breathe again — literally. There is something about being small inside something that enormous that puts everything in perspective. I grew up with anxiety and mountains were always where it quietened. Canmore holds a lot of my history. I am still figuring out what to do with all of it.

Alberta, Canada
London · 2025 — present

I moved here not entirely knowing why — only that something needed to shift, and sometimes you have to change the backdrop before you can change anything else. London has been hard and rich and full in the way that cities that don't care about you can be. I have found pockets of stillness in it. I am still finding them. My dog helps.
